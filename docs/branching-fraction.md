## $N^*$ baryons

### Evidence in the $\Lambda K$ channel

The [2026 PDG review of $N$ and $\Delta$ resonances](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-n-delta-resonances.pdf) assigns separate evidence ratings to the existence of each resonance and to its observation in individual decay channels. Table 80.1 gives the following ratings for the $N^*$ states considered in the $\Lambda K \pi$ model.

| $N(2250)$ | $\frac{9}{2}^-$ | ★★★★           | ★                     |

The channel-specific ratings indicate the strength of the evidence for observing a resonance in $\Lambda K$; they are not branching fractions and do not determine the amplitude couplings. In particular, a high overall resonance status does not imply strong evidence in $\Lambda K$. The ratings can therefore guide which resonances to test, but their couplings must remain free model parameters.
